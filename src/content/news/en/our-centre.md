Benedict Menni Psycho-Social Rehabilitation Centre, established in 2017, is a dedicated institution for the care and rehabilitation of mentally ill women. Functioning as a unit of the Trivandrum Social Service Society (TSSS), the centre is lovingly run by the Sisters Hospitallers of the Sacred Heart of Jesus Congregation. Guided by the Hospitaller spirit of compassion and service, the centre provides a safe, supportive, and holistic environment where residents can regain confidence, rebuild social and emotional skills, and gradually reintegrate into society as empowered individuals.
